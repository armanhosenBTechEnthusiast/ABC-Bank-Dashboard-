# 🏦 ABC Bank Customer & Balance Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes **ABC Bank's** customer demographics, balance distribution, and regional performance using **Power BI**. It provides deep insights into total balance, customer count, job classification, age distribution, gender breakdown, and monthly customer acquisition trends.

---

## 📈 Key Features of Dashboard

- 💰 **Total Balance, Total Customers & Top Customer** — KPI Summary Cards
- 👔 **Job Classification** — Pie/donut breakdown (White Collar, Blue Collar, Other)
- 💵 **Balance Distribution** — Bar chart by Balance Groups (0K–150K+)
- 🎂 **Age Distribution** — Bar chart by Age Groups (20s–60s)
- 📅 **New Customer by Month** — Monthly acquisition bar chart (Jan–Dec)
- ⚧ **Count of Customer ID by Gender** — Pie chart (Male vs Female split)
- 🗺️ **Region and Region** — Geographic map across England, Northern Ireland, Scotland & Wales

---

## 🧠 Insights Derived

- **Total Balance:** £162.34M | **Total Customers:** 4,010 | **Top Customer:** Dorothy Jackson
- **White Collar** customers are the largest job segment at **48.70%**, followed by Blue Collar (**26.11%**) and Other (**25.19%**)
- **Balance concentration** peaks in the 0K–50K range with 600–700 customers, declining sharply at higher bands
- **Age Group 30–40** holds the highest customer count, exceeding **800 customers**
- **Female** customers slightly dominate at **53.97% (2.16K)** vs Male at **46.03% (1.85K)**
- **New customer acquisition** peaks in the second half of the year, with highest numbers in **October–December**
- Customer presence is spread across all UK regions: **England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland**
